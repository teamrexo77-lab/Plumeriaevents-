About Plumeria

Planned with care,
celebrated together.

Plumeria Event Planners is an event planning team based in Mavelikara, Alappuzha, Kerala. From planning to execution, we bring stage and venue decor, catering, light and sound, studio coverage, cars, makeup, music and anchoring together for your celebration.

Booking is open for 2026–27, and we offer our services all over the country.
